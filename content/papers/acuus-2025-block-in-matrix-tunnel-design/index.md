abstract: >-
  This paper presents a methodology for modelling block-in-matrix rock
  ("bimrocks") in underground construction, combining a voxel-based
  geological model, a conditional random field parameter model, and finite
  element numerical simulation. The framework targets the internal
  heterogeneity and spatial variability that make bimrocks difficult to
  model with conventional geotechnical approaches, and is intended as a
  reliable, automated basis for tunnel design in such formations.
