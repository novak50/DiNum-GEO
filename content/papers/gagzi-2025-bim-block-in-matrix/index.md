abstract: >-
  This paper presents a workflow connecting ground models, Building
  Information Modelling (BIM), and finite element modelling (FEM).
  Borehole-based voxel models of block-in-matrix formations with matrix
  variability are imported into Revit, where Dynamo scripts enable
  visualization and cross-section extraction; geometry and material data
  are then exported to PLAXIS 2D via a Python API. The approach saves time
  and captures block-in-matrix heterogeneity more realistically than
  conventional homogeneous models.
