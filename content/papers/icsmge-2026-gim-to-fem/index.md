abstract: >-
  This paper presents a modelling framework that joins ground information
  modelling (GIM) with probabilistic finite element analysis for
  underground construction. A voxel-based 3D geological model is generated
  in a GIS environment, conditional random fields simulate the spatial
  distribution of geotechnical parameters, and the combined digital ground
  information model is exported to a finite element mesh through a BIM
  platform, with model updating as new data becomes available. The
  approach is demonstrated on a mechanized tunnelling case study.
