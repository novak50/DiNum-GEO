  This chapter applies a conditional random field-driven finite element
  approach to flood defense embankment design, extending the project's
  ground-modelling methodology from tunnelling to river embankments. It
  incorporates geotechnical parameter spatial variability into the
  numerical design process, aiming for a more realistic assessment of
  embankment performance than conventional deterministic approaches.
