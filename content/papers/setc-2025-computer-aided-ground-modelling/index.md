abstract: >-
  This paper presents an end-to-end workflow for generating a
  three-dimensional, voxel-based ground information model that integrates
  geological complexity and soil-property variability into finite element
  analyses. Lithology is interpolated via Empirical Bayesian Kriging in
  ArcGIS Pro, spatial variability of key geotechnical parameters is
  characterized with conditional random fields in GSTools, and the
  resulting model is imported into a CutFEM solver for adaptive finite
  element simulation, demonstrated on a synthetic excavation scenario.
