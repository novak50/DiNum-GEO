abstract: >-
  This paper examines how many investigation boreholes are needed, and how
  they should be distributed in space, to obtain reliable 3D ground models.
  Empirical Bayesian Kriging in a GIS environment is used to generate
  voxel-based lithological models from subsets of 10 to 70 boreholes in
  different spatial patterns. Prediction accuracy is evaluated against
  independent validation boreholes and a quasi-reference model based on all
  94 boreholes, with the standard error of prediction used as an uncertainty
  indicator. Accuracy increases and uncertainty decreases with more boreholes
  and a better spatial arrangement, especially where investigation coverage
  is poor.
